# ICT Killzones & Institutional Liquidity Windows

## Core Principle
Never treat all 24 hours equally. Institutional order flow, Stop Hunts (Liquidity Sweeps), and Fair Value Gap (FVG) displacements concentrate inside New York (EST/EDT) Killzone windows:

1. **London Open Killzone (`02:00 – 05:00 NY`)**
   - Sweeps the Asian Session High/Low liquidity pool.
   - High probability for daily high/low formation and SMC Order Block / FVG continuations.
2. **New York AM Open Killzone (`08:00 – 11:00 NY`)**
   - Peak Gold (`OANDA:XAUUSD`) & USD liquidity overlap with US macro releases (CPI, NFP, PPI, Fed speakers).
   - Highest conviction window for ICT Silver Bullet (`10:00 – 11:00 NY`) and BOS/CHoCH reversals.
3. **London Close / NY Lunch Transition (`11:00 – 13:00 NY`)**
   - Medium liquidity; watch for profit-taking reversals against the morning trend.
4. **Asian Range (`20:00 – 00:00 NY`) & Off-Session Chop**
   - Low institutional volume; avoid chasing breakouts. Mark Asian High/Low as liquidity targets for London.

## Guardrail
- When `ICT Killzones Only` is active on intraday timeframes (`1m`–`1h`), reject entries outside London/NY Killzones.
- When operating in `All Sessions`, downgrade off-session signals in the Bear Critic review if confidence is below 75%.
